# Chonky Catnap look mechanics

The belly, seated hips, feet, tail base, and correctly oriented keyboard remain anchored to the idle pose. The two paws rest on the board. Head yaw and pitch lead the gaze with mild cheek/ear/upper-shoulder follow-through, while the lower silhouette stays frontal. Eyes retain their white sclera, dark brown irises, dark outline, and tired orange lids; rotation exposes a little more sclera when needed, without replacing the eyes.

Up: chin and nose lift, eyes rotate toward the top edge, lower sclera visible. Down: chin and nose lower, eyelids angle downward, pupils at lower side of eye surfaces, gaze toward keyboard. Screen-right: nose and muzzle move right of head center with gentle three-quarter yaw, pupils toward right edge. Screen-left: opposite yaw and eye rotation. Face remains visible and recognizable at every angle. Whole-body turns, whole-sprite rotations, detached pupils, and broad raster warps are forbidden.

Intermediate directions combine physical pitch and yaw in even steps. Keep body, keyboard, feet and their scale constant, folded anatomical left ear (screen-right in frontal views), and tail on screen-right. Keyboard stays cat-facing: spacebar nearest belly, numpad screen-left, function row viewer-front, teal Escape bottom-right. No props, labels, shadows or effects.
